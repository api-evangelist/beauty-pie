The Tiny Technology Everyone’s Talking About. If you’ve been keeping even half an eye on the skincare world recently, you may have noticed one word appearing everywhere: spicules . They’re tiny, they tingle, and they represent a new shift in skincare thinking, namely a move away from thinking only about which ingredients are in a formula, towards thinking about how intelligently those ingredients are delivered to the skin .
